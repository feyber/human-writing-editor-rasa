# Example: Blog paragraph

## Input

```text
In today's rapidly evolving AI landscape, creators are leveraging cutting-edge tools to streamline their workflows and unlock new levels of productivity. This marks a pivotal moment for the creator economy.
```

## Issues found

- "In today's rapidly evolving AI landscape" is a formulaic opening.
- "leveraging cutting-edge tools" is generic AI vocabulary.
- "streamline their workflows" is vague unless the workflow is named.
- "unlock new levels of productivity" is inflated and unspecific.
- "pivotal moment" overstates the claim.

## Human Writing Editor output

Final:

```text
Creators are using AI to cut out the boring middle steps: rough drafts, first edits, captions, thumbnails, and repurposed clips. The useful tools are not magic. They just make the blank page less expensive.
```

Score: 42/50

- Directness: 8
- Rhythm: 8
- Trust: 9
- Authenticity: 8
- Density: 9

Why it works:

- It names the actual workflow.
- It avoids hype.
- It has a point of view without sounding like a press release.
