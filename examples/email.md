# Example: Email

## Input

```text
I hope this message finds you well. I wanted to reach out to explore a potential collaboration opportunity between our teams. I believe there may be significant synergies worth discussing.
```

## Human Writing Editor output

Final:

```text
I wanted to ask about a possible collaboration between our teams. Your audience overlaps with ours, and I think there is a simple campaign we could test together.
```

Score: 40/50

- Directness: 8
- Rhythm: 7
- Trust: 8
- Authenticity: 8
- Density: 9

Why it works:

- It cuts the greeting filler.
- It replaces "synergies" with a concrete reason.
- It makes the ask feel normal instead of corporate.
